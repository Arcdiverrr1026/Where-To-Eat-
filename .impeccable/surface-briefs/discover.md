## Direction contract
THESIS: Help choose a real nearby meal with a visible search scope and reasons; do not present a promotional landing page.
OWN-WORLD: Grounded candidates: neighborhood wayfinding, everyday menu typography, public-park wayfinding, grocery labels, city transit information, local directory, recipe index. Assigned candidate 3: park wayfinding translated only as forest-green shell, clear system typography, soft green selection and generous reading space. No literal signage decoration.
STORY: Choose a location and filters, search, compare real information, inspect a store, save or review it. Missing configuration and empty results are explicit.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact forest-green navigation, a clear question heading, then a 300px filter column and flexible result list. On phones filters precede results. Primary search action is directly below filters. Signature: each recommendation carries a compact, labeled reason line linked to the exact filter scope.
FORM: Candidate 3, public-park wayfinding; seed:bb080c0a898dadb1. Familiar forms, links, and list navigation stay native. Motion limited to a short results reveal, reduced-motion respected.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Palette strategy: forest-green navigation and tinted work surface; lime identifies the primary action. Physical scene: people choose lunch outdoors or at a desk under ordinary daylight, requiring a light, high-contrast work surface. No invented restaurant content.
